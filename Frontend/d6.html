COVID-19 Awareness

Coronavirus disease (COVID-19) is an infectious respiratory disease caused by the SARS-CoV-2 virus. Awareness and timely prevention are key to managing its impact.

How It Spreads

  • Respiratory droplets from coughs or sneezes.
  • Close contact with infected individuals.
  • Touching contaminated surfaces and then touching face.
  • Airborne transmission in poorly ventilated indoor spaces.

Prevention Steps

  • Wear a mask in crowded areas.
  • Wash hands frequently with soap.
  • Maintain safe physical distancing.
  • Avoid touching eyes, nose, and mouth.
  • Keep indoor spaces well ventilated.
