Human Metapneumovirus (HMPV)

HMPV is a significant respiratory virus that can cause upper and lower respiratory tract infections. While it affects people of all ages, it is most common in children, older adults, and individuals with weakened immune systems.

Symptoms of HMPV

  • Persistent Cough
  • Congestion or Runny Nose
  • Sore Throat
  • Fever
  • Shortness of Breath
  • Wheezing (Lower respiratory)

Prevention Tips

  • Frequent & Thorough Handwashing
  • Avoid Contact with Infected Persons
  • Cover Mouth/Nose when Sneezing
  • Disinfect Touched Surfaces
  • Stay Home when Symptomatic
