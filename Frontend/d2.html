Malaria: Symptoms & Care

Symptoms of Malaria

  • High Fever & Chills
  • Excessive Sweating
  • Severe Headache
  • Nausea & Vomiting
  • Muscle & Body Aches
  • General Fatigue (Malaise)

Prevention Strategies

  • Use insecticide-treated bed nets.
  • Apply mosquito repellent (DEET).
  • Wear protective long-sleeved clothing.
  • Remove standing water near homes.
  • Keep windows and doors screened.

Clinical Medical Treatment

  • Antimalarial Drugs: Core clinical therapy.
  • Antipyretics: To manage fever and pain.
  • IV Fluids: To maintain electrolyte balance.
  • Antiemetics: To control severe nausea.
  • Antibiotics: For secondary infections.

Official Health Guidelines: World Health Organization (WHO)
