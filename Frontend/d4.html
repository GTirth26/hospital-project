Gastric Disorders

Gastric disorders encompass a range of issues affecting the stomach and intestines. Common triggers include unhealthy dietary habits, chronic stress, smoking, or excessive alcohol intake. Proper management is essential to maintain digestive health and overall well-being.

Common Symptoms

  • Persistent bloating and gas
  • Localized stomach pain
  • Indigestion and discomfort
  • Frequent heartburn (Reflux)
  • Nausea or vomiting

Prevention Tips

  • Maintain a balanced, fiber-rich diet
  • Avoid excessive alcohol and tobacco
  • Reduce stress through mindfulness
  • Eat small, regular meals
  • Maintain adequate hydration

Diagnosis & Clinical Care

Clinical diagnosis involves a thorough review of medical history, physical examinations, and specialized tests. In some cases, imaging techniques like ultrasounds or endoscopies may be required to identify underlying issues such as ulcers or inflammation.

When to See a Specialist?

Consult a gastroenterologist immediately if you experience: Severe abdominal pain, blood in vomit or stool, persistent weight loss, or symptoms that last for more than two weeks despite home care.
