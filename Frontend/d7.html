Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease (COPD)

COPD is a progressive lung disease characterized by chronic inflammation and airflow limitation in the lungs. It makes breathing difficult and affects the quality of life for millions globally.

[Image of healthy lungs versus lungs affected by chronic bronchitis and emphysema]

Primary Symptoms

  • Shortness of breath (Dyspnea)
  • Chronic wheezing while breathing
  • Persistent chest tightness
  • Ongoing, productive cough
  • Frequent respiratory infections

Prevention & Risk Control

  • Quit smoking and avoid tobacco.
  • Minimize exposure to air pollution.
  • Avoid workplace chemical fumes.
  • Practice healthy respiratory hygiene.
  • Regular lung function screenings.
