Stroke Awareness & Care

A stroke is a medical emergency that occurs when blood flow to a part of the brain is interrupted or reduced, preventing brain tissue from getting oxygen and nutrients. Brain cells begin to die in minutes.

Types of Stroke

  • Ischemic Stroke: Caused by a blockage in an artery.
  • Hemorrhagic Stroke: Caused by a leaking or bursting blood vessel.
  • TIA (Mini-Stroke): A temporary period of symptoms similar to those of a stroke.

Warning Symptoms

  • Sudden numbness or weakness
  • Difficulty speaking or understanding
  • Sudden severe headache
  • Loss of balance or coordination
  • Vision problems in one or both eyes

Think "FAST" in Emergencies

If you suspect someone is having a stroke, act immediately:

  • F (Face): Ask the person to smile. Does one side droop?
  • A (Arms): Ask the person to raise both arms. Does one drift down?
  • S (Speech): Is their speech slurred or strange?
  • T (Time): If you see these signs, call emergency services NOW.
