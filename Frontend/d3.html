Influenza (Flu) Care

Influenza (flu) is a contagious respiratory illness caused by influenza viruses. It can range from mild to severe, and annual vaccination remains the most effective prevention strategy.

Common Symptoms

  • High fever (above 100.4°F)
  • Severe muscle & body aches
  • Persistent dry cough
  • Extreme fatigue & weakness
  • Nasal congestion & sore throat

Prevention Tips

  • Annual Flu Vaccination
  • Frequent Hand Sanitization
  • Respiratory Etiquette (Cover coughs)
  • Disinfect Common Surfaces
  • Avoid Contact with Infected Persons

Recommended Treatment

Most healthy individuals recover with rest, hydration, and over-the-counter fever reducers. However, for high-risk groups, doctors may prescribe antiviral medications like Oseltamivir (Tamiflu) to reduce the severity and duration of the illness.

Emergency Signs

Seek immediate medical care if you experience: Difficulty breathing, persistent chest pain, sudden confusion, or a fever that returns with a worse cough.
